Roadmap

AutoMate keeps growing. Here's what we're working on. No dates: we ship when it's right.

Coming next

Being designed or built now.

  • Trip collections

    Group trips into one journey, like “Goa, May 2026”, with combined distance, cost and one map.

  • Share card editor

    Choose what goes on a trip's share image, in story, square or wide sizes. Exact start and end hidden by default.

  • Your year on the road

    A monthly and yearly recap of kilometres, spend and places, with personal records.

  • Warranty tracking

    Standard and extended warranty by date or kilometres, with a reminder before it ends.

  • Business trip report

    Export tagged trips for a period as CSV or PDF, ready for reimbursement.

  • Running cost

    Fuel and garage spend in one view, with cost per km over time.

Exploring

Ideas we're testing. They may change or not happen.

  • Shared car

    Several drivers on one car, with trips, fuel and garage kept in step between phones.

  • Road quality

    Potholes and rough roads picked up by the phone's motion sensors and shown on the trip map.

  • All vehicles at a glance

    Distance and spend across every vehicle you own, in one place.
