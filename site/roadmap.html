Roadmap

Here’s what we’d like to bring to AutoMate next. Have an idea? We’d love to hear it.

What’s ahead

  • AutoMate for iPhone

    Bring trip tracking and vehicle records to iOS.

  • All your vehicles at a glance

    See distance and spending across your cars, bikes and scooters in one view.

  • One car, shared records

    Share a car with family? Keep everyone’s trips, fuel and service records together across phones.

  • Potholes and rough roads

    Spot rough patches using your phone’s motion sensors and see them on your trip map.

  • Crash detection

    Detect a possible crash, check if you’re okay, and alert your chosen emergency contact if you don’t respond.

  • Trip reports for reimbursements

    Export work trips and their costs as a PDF or spreadsheet.
