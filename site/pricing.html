Pricing questions

What counts as a trip?

Every trip AutoMate records, automatic or started by hand, across all your vehicles. Deleting a trip doesn't give it back.

What happens after trip 50?

Nothing is lost. New trips keep recording and are saved, but their route, times, places and cost stay locked until you pick a plan. Your first 50 trips stay open for good, and your km and mileage totals still count every trip. AutoMate tells you from trip 40, and once more at trip 50.

What if my monthly plan ends?

Trips recorded while it was active stay open. Only trips after it ends are locked, until you renew or buy Lifetime.

I'm on Monthly. Can I switch to Lifetime?

Yes. Buy Lifetime, then cancel Monthly in Google Play; the app links you straight there.

How do I cancel?

In Google Play: your profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → AutoMate. You keep Monthly until the end of the month you paid for.

Is the launch offer real?

Yes. Lifetime is ₹799 until 31 December 2026, 23:59 IST. After that it's ₹999. Monthly stays ₹69.

Can I take my data with me if I don't pay?

Yes. Export a backup of everything any time, on any plan. AutoMate never deletes your trips.
