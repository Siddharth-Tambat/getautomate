Terms of use

Plain-English terms for using AutoMate. By installing or using the app you agree to them.

Last updated 23 November 2026

Using AutoMate

  • AutoMate is free to install, and your first 50 trips are free. Expenses, fuel, FASTag and Garage stay free. After 50 trips, new trips are saved but locked until you buy a one-time purchase or a monthly subscription through Google Play. A subscription renews every month until you cancel it in Google Play. Refunds follow Google Play's policies.
  • You may use AutoMate for your own vehicles, personal or work.
  • Don't copy, resell, or reverse-engineer the app, or use it to break the law.

Not while driving

AutoMate works in the background so you don't have to touch your phone while driving. Don't use it while driving; set things up when parked. Obey traffic laws at all times.

Numbers are estimates

Distances, speeds, mileage, trip costs and reminders come from GPS, your entries and your payment notifications. They can be wrong: GPS drifts, alerts are missed or misread, and phones stop apps in the background. Don't rely on AutoMate for legal, tax, insurance, safety or maintenance decisions; check your vehicle's manual, odometer and official records.

Your data is your responsibility

Your records live only on your phone (see Privacy). If the phone is lost, reset or the app is uninstalled, your data is gone unless you exported a backup. A forgotten backup password can't be recovered.

Other services

Maps, addresses, routes and place lookups come from Google and follow Google's terms. FASTag, bank and UPI apps are not part of AutoMate; AutoMate only reads their notifications.

No warranty

AutoMate is provided “as is”, without warranty of any kind. To the extent the law allows, the developer isn't liable for any loss or damage from using it, including lost data, fines or costs.

Changes

These terms may change; the latest version is always on this page. Questions: hello@getautomate.in.
