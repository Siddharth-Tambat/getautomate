Setup guide

From install to your first automatic trip in about five minutes.

  • Install
  • Add your vehicle
  • Automatic trips
  • Payment notifications
  • Mileage
  • Reminders
  • New phone

1. Install

AutoMate is coming soon to Google Play. Once it's live, search for AutoMate and tap Install. You need Android 12 or newer. Your first 50 trips are free; nothing to fit in the vehicle.

2. Add your vehicle

Open AutoMate and tap Add vehicle. Pick car, bike or scooter, then the make and model; a nickname, number plate and colour are optional. Used AutoMate before? Tap Restore from a backup instead.

3. Turn on automatic trips

In the vehicle's details, choose its Bluetooth: the car stereo or hands-free kit, or the scooter's own Bluetooth. Then open Settings → Automatic trips and finish the checklist:

No Bluetooth on your bike? Start and end trips by hand from the Trips tab.

4. Payment notifications (optional)

In Settings, switch on Read payment notifications. AutoMate explains what it reads, then opens Android's Notification access page: turn on AutoMate. From then on FASTag tolls are logged as they happen, and fuel and other vehicle payments wait in To review for one tap. Choose which payments notify you under Settings → Notifications.

5. Your mileage

Settings → Mileage: enter the km/L you really get in the city (and on the highway, if it's different). Every trip is priced with it, and Home → Mileage shows km/L over time from your trips.

6. Service reminders

In the Garage tab, log your last service and set the date the next one is due. You'll get a notification a week before and the day before (or on the day, if you choose).

Moving to a new phone

Settings → Your data → Transfer to another phone → Export makes one .automate file with your vehicles, trips, fuel, garage log, photos and settings; protect it with a password if you like. Copy it to the new phone, install AutoMate and tap Restore from a backup.
