Simple. Smart. Private.

Your phone already knows when you drive. AutoMate puts that to work, so your trips and spending are logged automatically and every number is a tap away.

  • Runs on your phone. Your trips, bills and places are stored on your device, not on our servers. No account to create.
  • Uses the sensors you already carry. GPS and Bluetooth tell AutoMate when a drive starts, where it goes and when it ends.
  • Every vehicle, one app. Car, bike or scooter: switch between them and each keeps its own trips, costs and mileage.
  • Logs itself. Trips start and stop on their own, and fill-ups, tolls and parking come straight from your payment alerts.
  • All your data at your fingertips. Search any expense, see any month, share any trip.

Trips

Every drive, recorded on its own.

A trip starts when your phone connects to the vehicle's Bluetooth and ends seconds after you park and switch off.

  • GPS route on the map, with stops and a replay of the drive
  • City and highway kilometres, speed and time on the move
  • What the drive cost: fuel, tolls and parking
  • Tags like Office or Weekend; routes you tag often tag themselves
  • Edit, merge or unmerge trips, and share a trip image

Fuel

Fill-ups that log themselves.

Pay at the pump by UPI or card. AutoMate spots the payment notification, fills in the fill-up for you and you tap Save.

  • Reads payment notifications from your SMS, UPI and bank apps, on the phone only
  • Every trip priced at your own city and highway km/L
  • Mileage over time from how much city and highway you drive
  • Search every fill-up and expense, all the way back

Everything else for your vehicle

One app for what your car, bike or scooter needs and costs.

Garage and reminders

Service history with bills and job cards, and reminders by date or kilometres.

Expenses

Fuel, tolls, parking, fines and repairs: where each vehicle's money went, by day, week, month or year, with search.

FASTag tolls

Tolls logged from your payment alerts and matched to the trip, with a low-balance heads-up. Cars only.

Mileage

km/L for each of the last six months, from each trip's city and highway kilometres and your own two figures.

Stats over time

Distance, time, spend and trends by week, month, year or all time.

Move phones

One file, password-protected if you like, carries your vehicles, trips, bills and photos to a new phone.

A look inside

Dark, quick, one thumb. Shown with a made-up demo garage.
