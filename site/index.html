Trips

Your everyday trips. Automatically recorded.

Once configured, your vehicle’s Bluetooth connection starts the trip. AutoMate records the journey using your phone and saves it after you park.

  • GPS route on the map, with stops and a replay of the drive
  • City and highway kilometres, speed and time on the move
  • What the drive cost: fuel, tolls and parking
  • Tags like Office or Weekend; routes you tag often tag themselves
  • Edit, merge or unmerge trips, and share a trip image

Expenses

Fuel, FASTag and everyday vehicle costs.

Keep your vehicle spending together, with fuel payment detection and automatic FASTag tracking from supported issuer alerts.

  • Review and save fuel entries prepared from supported payment notifications
  • Track FASTag tolls, recharges and balance automatically from issuer alerts
  • Get low-balance warnings and start a recharge in AutoMate, then pay in your UPI app
  • Search fuel, tolls, parking and garage expenses for each vehicle

FASTag is available for cars. Payment features require setup.

Garage

Service history. Next service sorted.

Keep a record of services and repairs, with reminders for what’s due next.

  • Log services, repairs and other work for each vehicle
  • Keep bills, job cards, photos and PDFs with each entry
  • Set reminders by date or kilometres
  • Garage costs appear in Expenses too
