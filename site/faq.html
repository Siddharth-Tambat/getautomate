FAQ

Can't find your answer? Write to hello@getautomate.in.

Getting it

Where do I get AutoMate?

AutoMate is coming soon to Google Play. Updates will arrive through Play like any other app.

Which phones does it work on?

Android 12 or newer, with Google Play services (for maps and trip detection). There's no iPhone version.

Does it cost anything?

Your first 99 trips are free, and Expenses, fuel, FASTag and Garage are always free. After 99, new trips keep recording and are saved, but stay locked until you pick a plan: ₹999 once for life, or ₹69 a month, through Google Play. No ads and no sign-up either way. See pricing.

Do I need a tracker or other device?

No. AutoMate uses your phone and your vehicle's Bluetooth (the car stereo, hands-free kit or the scooter's own Bluetooth). Nothing to buy or fit.

Trips

How does a trip start on its own?

Choose your vehicle's Bluetooth (the car stereo, hands-free kit or the scooter's own Bluetooth) in the vehicle's details. When your phone connects to it, a trip starts; when you park and switch off, it ends. See How it works.

A trip didn't start or end on its own. Why?

Open Settings → Automatic trips and work through the checklist: Bluetooth on, location “Allow all the time”, battery “Unrestricted”. Some phones also stop background apps; search your phone's brand on dontkillmyapp.com. You can always start and end a trip by hand from the Trips tab.

I stopped for a few minutes. Will that split my trip?

No. If you drive off again within 15 minutes from the same spot, AutoMate carries on the same trip.

Does it drain the battery?

GPS runs only while a trip is recording. The rest of the time AutoMate waits for your vehicle's Bluetooth.

Does it work with bikes and scooters?

Yes. Trips, fuel, expenses, mileage and garage work the same. FASTag is hidden for two-wheelers. No Bluetooth on your bike? Start and end trips by hand.

Fuel and payments

How does a fill-up log itself?

With Read payment notifications on, a UPI or card payment at a fuel pump shows up as a fill-up ready to save, with the amount filled in. You add the litres or price if you like and tap Save. See How it works.

Does AutoMate read my SMS?

No. It doesn't have SMS permission. It reads payment notifications only from SMS, UPI, bank and FASTag apps and Gmail, on your phone, and ignores everything else. Details in Privacy.

Will it count my grocery or food payments?

No. AutoMate is for vehicle costs. Payments that aren't for the vehicle are skipped, and nothing counts as spend until you log it.

How is my mileage worked out?

You enter the mileage you really get, for the city and the highway. AutoMate prices trips with it, and once you've logged a few full-tank fill-ups it fine-tunes the figure slowly. You can switch that off in Settings → Mileage.

Your data

Do I need an account?

No. Open the app and add your vehicle.

Where is my data?

Only on your phone. There are no AutoMate servers. Maps, addresses and some place lookups come from Google; see Privacy.

How do I move to a new phone?

Settings → Your data → Transfer to another phone → Export makes one .automate file. Install AutoMate on the new phone and tap Restore from a backup.

What if I lose my phone?

Your data goes with it unless you exported a backup. There's no cloud copy.

How do I delete my data?

See Delete your data.

Can family members share one car?

Not yet. Each phone keeps its own records; there's no syncing between phones.

Contact

How do I report a bug or ask for a feature?

Write to hello@getautomate.in. In the app: Settings → Help & feature requests. A screenshot helps; leave out number plates and places you'd rather not share.
