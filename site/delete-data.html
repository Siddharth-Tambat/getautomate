Delete your data

You can delete your local AutoMate records anytime. Exported copies and Google Play order records are separate from the data in the app.

Last updated 22 November 2026

Delete some of it

  • A trip, fill-up, expense, toll or garage entry: open it and use Delete on its page (in the ⋮ menu or the edit form).
  • A vehicle and everything recorded for it: Settings → tap ✏ on the vehicle → the delete (bin) icon at the top. Its trips, fuel, expenses, tolls, garage log and reminders go with it.
  • A saved place: Settings → Places → open the place → Delete place.
  • Payments waiting for review: skip them in To review; anything you don't log is deleted on its own after 1, 3 or 7 days.
  • Payment notifications: switch off Read payment notifications in Settings to stop reading new ones.

Delete all of it

  1. Open Android Settings → Apps → AutoMate → Storage.
  2. Tap Clear storage (on some phones, Clear data). This deletes every vehicle, trip, record, photo and setting.

Or simply uninstall AutoMate; Android deletes its data with it.

This can't be undone. AutoMate has no cloud copy. If you might want your records later, export a backup first: Settings → Your data → Transfer to another phone → Export.

Backup files

An exported .automate file is yours: delete it wherever you saved or sent it (your Files app, Drive, chat).

Data held by Google

The developer holds no data about you. Map, address and place requests go to Google (see Privacy); you can manage what Google keeps in your Google Account.
